I am a fourth year PhD student in Computational and Applied Mathematics at the University of Chicago, where I am advised by Jeremy Hoskins. 

In my research, I use tools from applied and numerical analysis to study wave phenomena. I am especially interested in the analysis and simulation of waves in the presence of thin layer and boundary layer effects. The singular perturbations that often appear in problems of this kind give rise to singularities and separation of scales that make them both mathematically interesting and computationally challenging. These thin layer effects are of particular interest in the simulation and design of hearing aids and loudspeakers, for example.
